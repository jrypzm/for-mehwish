A letter, just for you.

Mehwish,

I don't know how to explain what you are to me without it sounding like a movie. So I'll just say it the way it is:

You are the best thing that ever walked into my life. And you didn't even knock. You just walked in, sat down, and made yourself the centre of everything — and I never once wanted you to leave.

And the funniest part is, you liked me first. You were the one who couldn't stop talking about me. You were the one who liked me for ages and never said a word. I only found out because a friend told me. And then you told me yourself.

You're the last thing I think about before I sleep and the first thing my brain runs to when I wake up. When something good happens, you're the first person I want to tell. When something's wrong, you're the only person I want to hear from.

I love your eyes and your smile and your hair and your voice. I love how angry you get. I love how you take care of me without even realising you're doing it. I love the way you type when you're excited. I love how you say my name.

But what I love most about you is that you're you. Not the idea of you. Not a version of you. You. Exactly as you are. Angry, cute, stubborn, soft, silly, and completely mine.

I want more nights talking until we're both too sleepy to keep going. I want to hear you laugh more. I want to make you blush more. I want to bother you for the rest of my life.

Happy one year, my love. Thank you for being my girl, my safe space, my headache, my favourite person, and the girl I'll keep choosing.

Forever yours,
Ghanim ♡
